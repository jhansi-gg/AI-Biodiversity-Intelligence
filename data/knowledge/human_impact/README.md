# TerraMind AI 🌱

## AI-Powered Biodiversity Intelligence System

TerraMind AI is an AI-powered environmental intelligence system designed to analyze ecosystem conditions and generate evidence-backed environmental recommendations.

The system combines environmental data, scientific knowledge, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), semantic embeddings, and multi-metric reasoning to identify environmental risks and suggest suitable management actions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Environmental conditions such as soil degradation, water stress, climate variation, land-use change, and biodiversity loss are interconnected.

Traditional environmental analysis often examines these factors separately.

TerraMind AI addresses this challenge by analyzing multiple environmental variables together and connecting them with scientific evidence.

---

## 💡 Solution

TerraMind AI analyzes:

- Soil organic carbon
- Soil pH
- Soil moisture
- Rainfall
- Temperature
- Crop diversity
- Land use
- Habitat diversity
- Biodiversity indicators
- Human impacts

The system identifies environmental risks and generates recommendations supported by retrieved scientific knowledge.

---

## 🧠 Key Features

### 1. Environmental Risk Detection

Identifies conditions such as:

- Low soil organic carbon
- Low rainfall
- High temperature
- Low crop diversity
- Land-use pressure

### 2. RAG-Based Knowledge System

The system retrieves relevant information from a structured environmental knowledge base.

Knowledge categories include:

- Soil Health
- Biodiversity
- Climate
- Land Use
- Human Impact
- Scientific Sources

### 3. Multi-Metric Reasoning

TerraMind AI connects multiple environmental variables.

Example:

```text
Low Soil Carbon
       +
Low Rainfall
       +
Monoculture
       ↓
Multiple Environmental Pressures
       ↓
Diversified Management
       ↓
Soil + Water + Habitat + Biodiversity Benefits
